Standard Examination Question Bank with detailed solutions, speed tricks, and fractional equivalents table.

📌 Problem Preview · Question 01

If the price of a commodity increases by 25%, by what percentage must a household reduce its consumption so that the total expenditure remains constant?

(A) 15%
(B) 20% ✓
(C) 22.5%
(D) 25%
💡 Key Concept & Shortcut Formula

For an increase of \(r\%\), reduction required in consumption = \(\left(\frac{r}{100 + r}\right) \times 100\%\) = \(\left(\frac{25}{125}\right) \times 100\% = 20\%\).
